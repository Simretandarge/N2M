What we are

{{ SITE_NAME }} (N2M) is a technology and innovation publication. We focus on clear, practical stories about AI, startups, products, and the digital economy, with a special eye on Africa and other emerging markets.

Mission

Our goal is to be a trusted guide for readers who want to understand how technology is changing work, business, and everyday life. We prefer slower, thoughtful coverage over chasing every headline.

What we cover

  • AI: How AI tools work in practice, what they mean for people and businesses, and where the risks are.
  • Startups: Founder stories, lessons from the ecosystem, and simple guides on building and growing products.
  • Reviews: Honest takes on products and services, with clear pros, cons, and who they are best for.
  • Opinion & analysis: Plain‑language commentary on innovation, policy, and the digital economy.

Meaning of “251”

“251” is part of our name and story. It signals “what’s next” and our focus on the next wave of technology, startups, and ideas shaping the region and the world.
